How to read these results
Support
The fraction of all transactions that contain the itemset. For example, support = 1.58% means the itemset appears in 1.58% of all baskets. Higher support = more common.
Confidence
Given the antecedent was purchased, the probability the consequent is also purchased. Confidence = 65% means that in 65% of baskets containing the antecedent, the consequent was also present.
Lift
How much more likely the consequent is purchased when the antecedent is present compared to random chance. Lift > 1 indicates a positive association (they co-occur more than expected), lift ≈ 1 means no relationship, and lift < 1 indicates a negative association.
